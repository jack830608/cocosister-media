Data Deletion Instructions / 資料刪除說明

App: cocosister.tw · Contact: cocosistertw@gmail.com · Last updated: 2026-09-02

English

This app has a single user (the owner of @cocosister.tw). The only data about other Threads users it keeps is a local reply log (post ID, username, our reply text, timestamp). To have any record about you deleted:

  1. Email cocosistertw@gmail.com with the subject “Data deletion” and your Threads username.
  2. We delete every matching record within 7 days and confirm by email.

If you are the app user and want to revoke the app's access to your account, remove the app from your Threads account settings; tokens then stop working and are deleted from the owner's device.

繁體中文

本應用程式只有一位使用者（@cocosister.tw 帳號主人）。關於其他 Threads 用戶，我們唯一保存的是本機回覆紀錄（貼文 ID、帳號名、我們的回覆內容、時間）。若要刪除與你有關的紀錄：

  1. 寄信到 cocosistertw@gmail.com，主旨「資料刪除」，附上你的 Threads 帳號名。
  2. 我們會在 7 天內刪除所有相符紀錄並回信確認。

若你是應用程式使用者本人想撤銷授權：在 Threads 帳號設定移除本應用程式，權杖即失效並自裝置刪除。
