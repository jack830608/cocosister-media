Privacy Policy / 隱私政策

App: cocosister.tw (Meta App ID 1858052132020927; Threads use case 1372303447867507) · Operator: the owner of Threads/Instagram account @cocosister.tw · Contact: cocosistertw@gmail.com · Last updated: 2026-09-02

English

What this app is. cocosister.tw is a single-user tool used by the owner of the Threads and Instagram account @cocosister.tw (a Taiwanese money-saving / deals account) to run that account: publish posts, read the account's own insights, read and answer replies and mentions, and search public Threads posts so the owner can reply from the owner's own account. There are no other end users.

Data we access via Meta APIs. Our own posts and their metrics; replies and mentions addressed to our account (post ID, username, text, timestamp); public Threads posts and public profile data returned by keyword search and profile lookup.

What we store. Only (a) our own posts' IDs and metrics, and (b) a local log of posts we replied to: post ID, author username, our reply text, timestamp — used solely to avoid replying to the same post twice. Search results and profile data are shown to the owner at decision time and are not stored. Access tokens are kept in a local, access-restricted file on the owner's computer.

What we do not do. We do not sell, rent, or share any data with third parties; we do not build profiles of other users; we do not use the data for advertising; we do not scrape.

Retention. The reply log is kept for 90 days, then pruned. Our own posts' metrics are kept for the account's own performance reviews.

Your rights / deletion. If you are a Threads user and want any record about you (e.g., a reply-log entry) removed, email cocosistertw@gmail.com with your username. We delete within 7 days. See also our Data Deletion Instructions.

Changes. Updates are posted on this page with a new date.

繁體中文

這是什麼。 cocosister.tw 是 Threads／Instagram 帳號「摳摳姐 @cocosister.tw」（台灣省錢好康分享帳號）帳號主人自用的工具：發文、看自己的成效數據、讀與回覆留言與提及、搜尋公開貼文以便用自己的帳號回覆。沒有其他使用者。

透過 Meta API 取用的資料。 我們自己的貼文與數據；對我們帳號的留言與提及（貼文 ID、帳號名、內文、時間）；關鍵字搜尋與帳號查詢回傳的公開貼文與公開個人檔案資料。

我們保存什麼。 只有 (a) 我們自己貼文的 ID 與數據，(b) 一份本機的回覆紀錄：貼文 ID、作者帳號名、我們的回覆內容、時間——僅用於避免重複回覆同一則。搜尋結果與帳號資料只在決定回覆當下顯示給帳號主人，不保存。存取權杖保存在帳號主人電腦上受限存取的檔案內。

我們不做的事。 不出售、不出租、不與第三方分享任何資料；不建立其他用戶的輪廓；不用於廣告；不爬取。

保存期間。 回覆紀錄保留 90 天後清除；自己貼文的數據用於帳號成效檢討而保留。

你的權利／刪除。 任何 Threads 用戶若希望移除與你有關的紀錄（例如回覆紀錄），請寄信到 cocosistertw@gmail.com 並附上帳號名，我們會在 7 天內刪除。另見資料刪除說明。
